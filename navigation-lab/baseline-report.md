## Interpretation limits

Times and speed tradeoffs are abstract assumptions. Success means a planner found a route on its grid. Failure is not proof of physical infeasibility. All planners know the complete map. Collision counts and energy are unmeasured (null), not zero. There is no friction, stiffness, sensor, learned-policy, actuator, or hardware model.

Conditional means use each robot's successful cases and must not be treated as matched comparisons. summary.json also includes paired fixed-compact/adaptive outcomes; timeSaved is computed only when both succeed. A positive value favors adaptive.
