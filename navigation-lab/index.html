What this can tell us

All robots know the entire map and use the same shortest-time search. Fixed robots have radii 8, 14, and 22; the reconfigurable robot may choose among those footprints. It starts in any allowed shape at no cost. Obstacles are checked against the full footprint, including during resizing.

With equal movement speeds, a fixed compact robot should match the reconfigurable robot. Any advantage under the speed tradeoff depends on that assumption. This is a navigation baseline—not evidence that origami hardware can crawl, resize, or transfer these results to the real world.
